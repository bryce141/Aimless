GUIDELINE 2.1(a) — nothing happened when tapping Generate

When Generate is tapped before the app has a location fix, build 1.0 (4) was
supposed to display an alert explaining what was needed. That alert could not
appear, for two reasons that compound:

1. iOS does not present an app's alert while another alert is already on
   screen. The system location permission prompt is on screen during exactly
   the first seconds of first launch — which is when Generate is first tapped.
   Our alert was requested during that window and was discarded.

2. The app tracked "an explanation is owed" as a single true/false flag. Once
   it had been set and the alert was discarded, the flag stayed set, so every
   later tap set it to the value it already held. That is not a change, so the
   app never asked to present anything again. From that point the button was
   silent for as long as the screen stayed open.

We reproduced this on an iPad Air simulator running iPadOS 26, in both of the
states a reviewer would be in — permission not yet answered, and permission
declined. In both, the first tap and every tap after it produced nothing
whatsoever on screen. That is the behavior you reported, and we are sorry it
survived into a second review.


WHAT CHANGED IN 1.0 (5)

We did not repair the alert. We removed the app's dependence on alerts for this
entirely, and changed what a tap does.

- A tap made before the app has a location fix is no longer refused. The app
  now remembers it, shows "Finding you..." with a progress indicator on the
  button itself, and generates automatically the moment the fix arrives. In the
  case you hit — tapping immediately on first launch — the result is now three
  routes rather than nothing. This matters most on iPad: Wi-Fi-only iPad models
  have no GPS receiver and derive position from nearby Wi-Fi networks, so the
  wait between opening the app and having a usable position is routinely
  several seconds.

- If a fix genuinely cannot be obtained within 15 seconds, or if location access
  is off, the app explains that in a message drawn directly on the screen,
  above the button, with a Try Again or Open Settings control. Ordinary on-screen
  content draws correctly underneath a system prompt, where an alert cannot.

- Every tap now produces an immediate visible change and haptic feedback. There
  is no longer any state in which tapping Generate does nothing.

We also improved a second failure that could look the same to a tester. Our
routing provider reduced the daily request allowance on our account by a factor
of ten earlier this week. When that allowance is exhausted, route generation
fails, and the app previously reported that in small text that was easy to miss.
Such failures are now shown in the same prominent message as everything else,
and we have moved routing for the region your review devices have been in onto
our own infrastructure, which has no such allowance.
